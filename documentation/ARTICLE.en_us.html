This is convesion of the Metafont outlines from the "ethiop" LaTeX package v0.7, developed by Berhanu Beyene, Manfred Kudlek, Olaf Kummer, and Jochen Metzinger. Conversion was performed with the [mf2ff.py utility](https://github.com/mf2vec-dev/mf2ff). The converted glyphs were refined and kerned by type designer Abraham Fikadu. Abraham also extended the glyph set to cover updates to the Unicode standard for Ethiopic since the last release of the ethiop package.

To contribute, see github.com/raeytype/ethiop.
